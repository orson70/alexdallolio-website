Who

Alessandro Dallolio was born in Turin and spent part of his childhood in Asia. He has always made images: drawing, painting, collage, then cinema, advertising, installations, artificial intelligence. He has worked with Nike, Lavazza, Prada, BMW, Disney, and with Webuild for eleven years. He lives and works in Milan.

Clients: Prada · Nike · BMW · Volkswagen · Bugatti · Ray-Ban · Timberland · Converse · Diadora · FILA · Colmar · Canali · John Richmond · Jacob Cohen · Luisa Spagnoli · Polaroid · Lavazza · Tetra Pak · Allianz · Novartis · Sisal · TIM · Chopard · Disney · Webuild.
Agencies: DLV, Young & Rubicam, JWT, Leo Burnett, Armando Testa. TV: Mediaset, Rai, Sky, Fox.
