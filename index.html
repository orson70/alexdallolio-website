The Approach

A tailor who works
with any fabric.

Most productions begin with a location, a crew and a shooting schedule. Mine often begins with what a company already has: archive footage, product images, technical material, renders, photographs, fragments.

Direction is not only what happens on set. It is the ability to see structure, rhythm, tension and meaning inside raw material.

No shoot required

I work remotely with existing assets. No travel, no production days, no location permits.

AI-native workflow

Generative AI is used as part of the visual process: to extend, reframe, imagine and build what the archive cannot provide.

Director's eye

Concept, editing, rhythm, music and visual treatment turn scattered material into something people can actually watch.

Works anywhere

Based in Milan, working globally. If the material exists, geography matters less.

Alessandro Dallolio was born in Turin and spent part of his childhood in Asia — India, Indonesia, Malaysia.

He has been making images for as long as he can remember. First drawing, painting, collage. Then cinema, advertising, video installations, artificial intelligence.

His method hasn't changed in thirty years: he takes material that doesn't seem to have a soul — corporate archives, found footage, generated images — and assembles it following a musical intuition rather than a visual logic. The result is recognisable before it can be explained.

He has worked with Nike, Lavazza, Prada, BMW, Disney. With Webuild for eleven years. The work he does for companies uses the language of advertising as a Trojan horse — to smuggle in something harder to define, and harder to forget.

Declared influences: Kubrick, Fellini, Buñuel, Gilliam. The common thread: disturbance as method, not effect.

He lives and works in Milan.
