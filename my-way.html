Alex Dallolio  /  My way
Six films · the doubt, then the film

My way.

A client usually wants the brand to be seen, big, and fair enough, they're paying... but I always end up looking for something else, the thing you don't see. For films like these there used to be the image bank: other people's pictures, good for everyone, so you had to stay vague, emotional. Today I can be precise, your own things, and I can take out what's no longer needed, the offices, the people at their computers. Everyone uses artificial intelligence by now. So what changes? Maybe only the way of looking, I think.

01

FILA F-Box

FILA · Salone del Mobile 2023

Fifty years of FILA, Design Week, and the logo, the F-Box. It was 2023, a world where artificial intelligence still couldn't do lettering, you asked for an F and got something else back... and the whole job was about a logo. That was the intuition: if I can build the possibility of that lettering, with an AI at its very beginnings, then I can tell stories. A time-lapse technique, a different story each time, but always starting from the logo.

02

FILA FW26

FILA · Milan Fashion Week

Five films for the collection, Milan Fashion Week. Three I shot on set, the way it has always been done. The other two I made without shooting a single frame, and there was only one doubt: next to the others, will the seam show? I think the trick is not to change your eye just because the tool changes. FILA published them on its own profiles. The two without a set are the ones above, but that's cheating, I just told you.

03

Fake / Egoïste

Personal research · 2026

No client here, I asked it of myself. A homage to Égoïste by Jean-Paul Goude, which was a huge production, a location built for it, dozens of performers, a chorus... Can you redo the spirit of something like that with no set and no crew? I still don't know, maybe not. But it came out, built one shot at a time, and in the end the work was the usual one: choosing, cutting, giving it rhythm.

04

Custodiamo il domani

Transpack Group · Corporate

A group that has been making paper and wood packaging for more than fifty years, and a film that had to be institutional, very institutional. Here I wanted to be precise: huge crates filmed as if from a crane, the port, their own things. I made it a year ago, when artificial intelligence wasn't what it is now, and it drove me a little crazy: the real where it existed, the rest generated, mixed together. No offices, no people at their computers... I tried to make a company that sells to other companies speak like a brand, with a kind of philosophy of its own, a language of its own.

05

Leonardo

Webuild · 2024

An exhibition on Leonardo in Australia, and Webuild wanting to tie Leonardo the engineer to the works it builds today. It all started from a letter, the one in which he offers himself to Ludovico il Moro, which in a way is the first CV in history: it lists everything he can do, bridges, canals, buildings... which happen to be the same things Webuild does. I no longer remember whether the idea was ours or the client's. The trouble was that the lead has been dead for five hundred years and what's left of him is drawings and codices, not footage. But there was first-generation AI, still rough, and for one thing it was the best system: taking Leonardo's drawings and turning them into Webuild's works. A red thread between the ancient world and the modern one... and Italians are the keepers of that knowledge, I think.

06

WeSearch 2025

Webuild · End-of-year film

The group's end-of-year film: awards, sites, new contracts, numbers, a whole year in two minutes. The client needs those things exact, they are coordinates, figures, often financial, and you have to give them exactly. The line was theirs, «In 2024 we searched for…». But a list of figures is still a slide... so I tried to fuse two things: graphics that give them exactly their points, and a journey, with elements inside that carry the story forward without weighing it down. Or at least I tried.
